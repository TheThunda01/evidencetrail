## Remaining limits

This is a targeted scientific and editorial audit, not an exhaustive systematic review or proof that every possible issue has been removed. Abstract-only appraisal cannot resolve every methodological detail. Browser hosting, permissions, real-review usability, accessibility across devices, and research effectiveness require evaluations specific to the intended setting. The prototype still does not read source reports, establish certainty of evidence, or provide a durable authenticated decision history.
